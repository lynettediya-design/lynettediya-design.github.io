02 / A little about me

Curious by nature.
Creative by choice.

I’m interested in how design can carry a feeling, make people pause, and turn an idea into something tangible.

I want my work to reach people — not just fill a screen.

I'm Lynette Diya, a student and emerging creative working across graphic design, branding and visual storytelling. I like experimenting with typography, colour, imagery and concepts until the pieces feel like they belong to one clear idea.

My goal is to create work that is thoughtful, expressive and useful: design that communicates honestly, connects with people, and gives everyday things a little more meaning.

This portfolio is a growing record of what I'm learning, making and discovering. I'm always looking for ways to turn curiosity into visual work.
